# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — as a contributor, issue reporter, or
discussion participant — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, issues, and reviews.
- Focus criticism on the code and ideas, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time; a slow or "no" response isn't
  hostility.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal/political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a GitHub issue
(for non-sensitive matters) or by contacting the maintainer directly via
their GitHub profile ([@Vincent6785](https://github.com/Vincent6785)) for
anything that shouldn't be public. All reports will be reviewed and
addressed as promptly as a small, independently maintained project allows.
Maintainers who violate this Code of Conduct are held to the same standard.

Possible consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces, at the maintainer's discretion,
proportionate to the severity of the behavior.
